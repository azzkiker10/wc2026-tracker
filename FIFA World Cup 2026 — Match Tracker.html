HOW TO USE

LIVE DATA · AUTO-SAVE · DUAL THEME

LIVE DATA SYNC

Scores are fetched automatically from openfootball on GitHub — a free, open-source, no-API-key data source maintained in real time during the tournament. The tracker polls for updates every 3 minutes. Hit ↻ REFRESH to sync immediately.

MANUAL SCORES

You can also type scores directly into the gold input boxes. Your manual entries are auto-saved to your browser and merge with any live data. A green border on an input means the score came from the live feed.

PENALTY SHOOTOUTS

For knockout matches decided on penalties, enter the full-time score (e.g. 1–1) then pick H or A in the P dropdown to record the shootout winner.

AUTO-SAVE

All manually entered scores are saved to your browser's localStorage automatically — 600ms after each keystroke. They survive page refreshes and come back on your next visit. A pulse dot confirms the system is active.

LIGHT / DARK MODE

Toggle between the dark stadium theme and the warm parchment light theme using the pill button at the top right of the navigation bar. Your preference is saved and restored on your next visit.

COLOUR KEY

Gold border — editable score field
Green border — auto-filled from live feed
Green bar — advances to Round of 32
Blue bar — potential best 3rd place

DATA SOURCE

Live scores: openfootball/worldcup.json on GitHub — free, open-source, no API key required. Schedule base: FIFA / Al Jazeera & USA TODAY draw coverage (Dec 2025). Times in Cairo (GMT+3).
